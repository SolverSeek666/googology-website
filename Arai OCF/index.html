Section 3: Ordinals for \(\Pi_N\)-Collection

In this section up to subsection 3.2, we work in a set theory \(\mathsf{ZFC}(\{St_i\}_i)\), where each \(St_i (0\lt i\le N)\) is a unary predicate symbol.

Let \(St_0\) denote the set of uncountable cardinal below \(\mathbb{I}_N\)
\(\Omega\) and \(\mathbb{I}_N\) are strongly critical numbers with \(\Omega\lt\mathbb{I}_N\), i.e., non-zero ordinals closed under the binary Veblen function \(\varphi\alpha\beta=\varphi_\alpha(\beta)\).
We assume that \(St_{i+1}\subset St_i\;\mathrm{for}\;i\lt N\), each \(St_i\) is an unbounded class of ordinals below \(\mathbb{I}_N\) such that the least element of \(St_i\) is larger than \(\Omega\), \(\Omega\lt\min(\bigcup_{0\lt i\le N} St_i)\).
The predicate \(St_i\) is identified with the class \(\{\alpha\in ON:\alpha\in St_i\}\).
\(\alpha^{\dagger i}\) denotes the least ordinal \(\gt\alpha\) in the class \(St_i\) when \(\alpha\lt\mathbb{I}_N\).
\(\alpha^{\dagger i}:=\mathbb{I}_N\) if \(\alpha\ge\mathbb{I}_N\).
Put \(\alpha^{\dagger}:=\alpha^{\dagger 1}\).
Let \(SSt_i:=\{\alpha^{\dagger i}:\alpha\in ON\}\) and \(LSt_i=St_i\;\backslash\;SSt_i\)

\(\Gamma_a\) denotes the \(a\)-th strongly critical number.
For ordinals \(\alpha\), \(\varepsilon(\alpha)\) denotes the least epsilon number above \(\alpha\), and \(\Gamma(\alpha)\) the least strongly critical number above \(\alpha\)
For ordinals \(\alpha,\beta\), and \(\gamma\), \(\gamma=\alpha-\beta\) designates that \(\alpha=\beta+\gamma\).
\(\alpha\dotplus\beta\) denotes the sum \(\alpha+\beta\) when \(\alpha+\beta\) equals to the commutative (natural) sum \(\alpha\#\beta\), i.e., when either \(\alpha=0\) or \(\alpha=\alpha_0+\omega^{\alpha_1}\) with \(\omega^{\alpha_1+1}\gt\beta\).

\(u,v,w,x,y,z,\dots\) range over sets in the universe, \(a,b,c,\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta,\dots\) range over ordinals \(\lt\varepsilon(\mathbb{I}_N)\), and \(\xi,\zeta,\eta,\dots\) range over ordinals less than \(\Gamma(\mathbb{I}_N)\), and ordinals \(\le\mathbb{I}_N\) are denoted by \(\pi,\kappa,\rho,\sigma,\tau,\lambda,\dots\).

Let \(\mathbb{S}\in St_i\) with \(i\gt 0\).
A 'Mahlo degree' \(m(\pi)\) of ordinals \(pi\lt\mathbb{S}\) with higher reflections is defined to be a finite function \(f:\mathbb{I}_N\rightarrow\Gamma(\mathbb{I}_N)\).
Let \(\Lambda\le\mathbb{I}_N\) be a strongly critical number.
To denote ordinals \(\lt\Gamma(\mathbb{I}_N)\), it is convenient for us to introduce an ordinal function \(\tilde{\theta}_1(\xi;\Lambda)=\Lambda^\xi\) with the base \(\Lambda\).

Definition 3.1

Let \(\Lambda\le\mathbb{I}_N\) be a strongly critical number.
\(\varphi_b(\xi)\) denotes the binary Veblen function on \((\mathbb{I}_N)^{\dagger 0}=\omega_{\mathbb{I}_N+1}\) with \(\varphi_0(\xi)=\omega^\xi\), and \(\tilde{\varphi}_b(\xi;\Lambda):=\varphi_b(\Lambda\cdot\xi)\).

Let \(b,\xi\lt (\mathbb{I}_N)^{\dagger 0}\).
\(\theta_b(\xi)[\tilde{\theta}_b(\xi;\Lambda)]\) denotes a \(b\)-th iterate of \(\varphi_0(\xi)=\omega^\xi\) [of \(\tilde{\varphi}_0(\xi;\Lambda)=\Lambda^\xi\)], resp.
Specifically ordinals \(\theta_b(\xi),\tilde{\theta}_b(\xi;\Lambda)\lt (\mathbb{I}_N)^{\dagger 0}\) are defined by recursion on \(b\) as follows.
$$\begin{align} \theta_0(\xi) = \tilde{\theta}_0(\xi;\Lambda) & = \xi \\ \theta_{\omega^b}(\xi) & = \varphi_b(\xi) \\ \tilde{\theta}_{\omega^b}(\xi;\Lambda) & = \tilde{\varphi}_b(\xi;\Lambda) \\ \theta_{c\dotplus\omega^b}(\xi) & = \theta_c(\theta_{\omega^b}(\xi)) \\ \tilde{\theta}_{c\dotplus\omega^b}(\xi;\Lambda) & = \tilde{\theta}_c(\tilde{\theta}_{\omega^b}(\xi;\Lambda);\Lambda) \end{align}$$
