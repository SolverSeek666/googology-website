Section 1: The Beginnning

\(\Omega\) denotes the least recursively regular ordinal \(\omega_1^{CK}\)
\(\psi_\Omega\) is a collapsing function such that \(\psi_\Omega(\alpha) \lt \Omega\)
\(\mathbb{I}_N\) is an ordinal term denoting an ordinal such that \(L_{\mathbb{I}_N} = \mathrm{KP}\omega+\Pi_N\text{-Collection}+(V=L)\)
Each \(St_i \; (0 \lt i \le N)\) is a unary predicate symbol
Let \(St_0\) denote the set of uncountable cardinals less than \(\mathbb{I}_N\), which is just
$$St_0 = \{\gamma|\gamma\in\mathrm{Card}\land\gamma\lt\mathbb{I}_N\}$$
\(\Omega\) and \(\mathbb{I}_N\) are strongly critical numbers with \(\Omega \lt \mathbb{I}_N\), in which strongly critical numbers are just gammas
We assume \(St_{i+1} \subset St_i\) for \(i \lt N\), and \(\Omega \lt \min\left( \bigcup_{0\lt i\le N} St_i \right)\)
For \(\alpha\lt\mathbb{I}_N\), \(\alpha^{\dagger i} := \min\{\gamma|\gamma\in St_i\land\gamma\gt\alpha\}\)
For \(\alpha\ge\mathbb{I}_N\), \(\alpha^{\dagger i} := \mathbb{I}_N\)
Let \(\alpha^\dagger := \alpha^{\dagger 1}\)
Let \(SSt_i := \{\alpha^{\dagger i}:\alpha\in\mathrm{Ord}\}\) and \(LSt_i = St_i\backslash SSt_i\)
\(\varepsilon(\alpha)\) denotes the least epsilon number greater than \(\alpha\)
\(\Gamma(\alpha)\) denotes the least gamma number greater than \(\alpha\)
\(\gamma = \alpha-\beta\) means \(\alpha = \beta+\gamma\) (tbh this is unnecessary sometimes)
\(\alpha\dotplus\beta\) denotes the sum \(\alpha+\beta\) when \(\alpha+\beta\) equals the commutative (natural) sum

Let \(\mathbb{S}\in St_i\) with \(i\gt 0\)
